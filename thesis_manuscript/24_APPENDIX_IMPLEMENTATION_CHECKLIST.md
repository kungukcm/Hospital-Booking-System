# APPENDIX E. IMPLEMENTATION, TESTING, AND SUBMISSION CHECKLIST

## 1. Purpose

This checklist is intended to help finalize the thesis package and align the manuscript with the implementation artifacts in the project repository.

## 2. Thesis Assembly Checklist

1. Confirm front matter follows the template structure.
2. Ensure chapter order matches institutional requirements.
3. Insert figure and table numbering consistently.
4. Harmonize heading styles and numbering format.
5. Apply final page-number conventions.

## 3. Content Completion Checklist

1. Confirm all chapters have complete introductions and summaries.
2. Ensure every research question is addressed in discussion and conclusion.
3. Verify that each chapter supplement is integrated logically.
4. Add missing empirical values, if any, from final pilot or tests.
5. Finalize definitions and abbreviations.

## 4. Reference Checklist

1. Replace all draft citations with final APA 7 entries.
2. Remove duplicate source records.
3. Verify titles, authors, years, and DOI links.
4. Confirm in-text citations match reference list entries.
5. Use hanging indent and consistent capitalization.

## 5. Figure and Table Checklist

1. Ensure every listed figure exists in the final document.
2. Ensure every listed table is labeled and referenced in text.
3. Keep numbering consistent across main chapters and supplements.
4. Update captions to reflect final content accurately.

## 6. Technical Artifact Checklist

1. Validate booking flow from start to confirmation.
2. Validate Swahili booking confirmation output.
3. Validate best-slot message localization.
4. Validate cancellation, next-appointment, and conflict checks.
5. Validate rate-limit and fallback behavior.

## 7. Governance Checklist

1. Confirm non-clinical scope statement is visible.
2. Confirm data protection and retention assumptions are documented.
3. Confirm escalation process is specified.
4. Confirm multilingual support is framed as inclusion and accessibility.
5. Confirm deployment recommendations include human oversight.

## 8. Final Proofreading Checklist

1. Remove placeholder names and bracketed fields.
2. Standardize apostrophes, hyphens, and capitalization.
3. Check for duplicated section headings.
4. Review citation consistency.
5. Verify tone remains academic and coherent.

## 9. Submission Readiness Checklist

1. Export the merged thesis document to Word/PDF.
2. Verify margins, line spacing, and page numbering.
3. Confirm appendix order and reference pages.
4. Review against departmental or advisor instructions.
5. Archive source files and final submission version separately.

## 10. Operational Checklist for Final Demo

1. Booking flow in English.
2. Booking flow in Swahili.
3. Best slots in English.
4. Best slots in Swahili.
5. Appointment cancellation and retrieval.
6. Error handling demonstration.

## 11. Closing Note

This checklist is intended to reduce last-mile submission errors and help ensure that the thesis package is consistent, traceable, and ready for final formatting.
