## APA Drafting Notes

For each source, verify:

1. authorship order and spelling;
2. publication year;
3. article or chapter title capitalization;
4. journal/proceedings/book title;
5. volume, issue, and page numbers;
6. DOI or URL if available;
7. duplicates across versioned files.

## Sample Final APA Structure

Author, A. A., Author, B. B., & Author, C. C. (Year). Title of the article. Title of Journal, volume(issue), pages. DOI

For proceedings:

Author, A. A. (Year). Title of paper. In Editor, E. E. (Ed.), Proceedings title (pp. xx-xx). Publisher/Conference.

For reports or white papers:

Organization Name. (Year). Title of report. Publisher or URL.

## Suggested Final Sorting Order

Alphabetize by first author surname after metadata verification.
